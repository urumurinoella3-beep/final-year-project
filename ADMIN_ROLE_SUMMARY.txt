┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKFLOW EXAMPLE                                                         │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

ADMIN REVIEWS AND PROVIDES GUIDANCE:

1. Admin views all issues (system-wide)
   ↓
2. Admin notices unassigned critical issue
   ↓
3. Admin clicks issue to view details
   ↓
4. Admin sees:
   • Status: OPEN (read-only badge)
   • Priority: HIGH (read-only badge)
   • Assigned To: Unassigned (read-only text)
   ↓
5. Admin writes comment:
   "HOD, this is critical. Please assign to senior staff immediately."
   ↓
6. HOD receives comment notification
   ↓
7. HOD reviews issue
   ↓
8. HOD assigns to senior staff member
   ↓
9. Staff receives assignment notification
   ↓
10. Staff works on issue
