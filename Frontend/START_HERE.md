> **[Point to User]**

> - ADMIN has full system access
> - HOD can assign issues to team members
> - Secretary handles data entry
> - Member resolves assigned issues
>
> **[Point to Issue]**  
> Issue is the main entity tracking data quality problems from creation to resolution. It has severity levels, status, and SLA deadlines.
>
> **[Point to IssueCategory]**  
> IssueCategory classifies issues into types like Data Quality, System Error, or Integration Problem.
>
> **[Point to IssueComment]**  
> IssueComment enables team collaboration and documents the resolution process.
>
> **[Point to relationships]**  
> The relationships show that one Department has many Users, one User reports many Issues, and one Issue has many Comments.
>
> **[Gesture to handouts]**
